Terms of Service

1. Your account

You need an account to use Konek. Give accurate information, keep your password private, and don't share your account. You are responsible for activity that happens under your account.

2. Location sharing

Konek shows your location to the circle (squad) you are in while location sharing is on. Only join circles you trust. You can stop sharing at any time from the map or Settings.

3. Acceptable use

Don't use Konek to track someone without their consent, harass or stalk another person, impersonate anyone, or break the law. We may suspend or remove accounts that do.

4. Content

You keep ownership of the profile picture and other content you add. Only upload content you have the right to use, and nothing harmful or offensive.

5. Service availability

Konek is provided as-is. Features may change, and location updates can be delayed by your device, network, or battery settings. Don't rely on Konek as your only safety tool in an emergency.

6. Ending your account

You can stop using Konek at any time. To delete your account and its server data, contact us at konek.application@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account details: username, email address, and a hashed password.
  • Optional details: contact number and profile picture.
  • Google sign-in identifier, if you choose Google.
  • Circle membership and roles.
  • Location updates and geofence activity while sharing is on.

How we use it

  • Sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • Show your position to the circles you belong to.
  • Send safety alerts, welcome mail, and password-reset links.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, and improve Konek.

Who sees your data

Your username, profile picture, and current location are visible to members of your circles. We don't sell your data. We only share it with service providers that help deliver the app (for example, an email provider used to send account mail).

Retention

Location history stays on the Konek server while your account is active so your circles can see recent activity. You can clear history stored on your device from Settings. Deleting your account removes your server data.

Your choices

You control when location sharing is on and which circles you join. You can update your profile in Settings, and you can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Contact

Questions about your data? Email konek.application@gmail.com.

By creating a Konek account you confirm that you have read and agree to these terms and this privacy policy.
